Signs a yard needs grading.

You do not have to know what the fix is. Send a photo of the problem spot and we will take it from there.

  • Water that sits in the same low spot after every rain.
  • Ruts and humps left behind by trucks or a build.
  • Bare, uneven ground where you want to put down seed or sod.
  • A yard that is hard to mow because it dips and rolls.

What a grading job involves.

We look at your photos, then at the yard. We work the existing ground: knock down the high spots, fill the low ones and smooth it all out, so you are left with a clean, finished grade. The work area is cleaned up when the job is done.

Ready for seed or sod.

A finished grade is the step before new grass. When we are done, the soil is ready for the next step, whether that is seed or sod. Got a stump in the way? We grind those too.
