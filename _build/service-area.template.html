The six cities.

Stump grinding, yard grading, light excavation and cleanup in all six. The notes are about the drive, since the work is the same wherever the stump is.

Virginia Beach

Home base. Davis Landworks is a Virginia Beach company, and the review below came from a Virginia Beach job: a few stumps around underground power lines, ground out and the garden bed left ready to plant.

“Had a few tree stumps to grind in Virginia Beach around some underground power lines. Harrison was able to carefully remove the tree stumps.”
James H, Google review

Chesapeake

Chesapeake shares a line with Virginia Beach and runs west to Suffolk and south to the North Carolina line. A job there can be a city lot or something with a lot more room. Text the address and a photo of the way in so we know what we are driving to.

Norfolk

Norfolk sits just north of Chesapeake and across the Elizabeth River from Portsmouth. Older city lots can be tight, so a photo of the gate or the side yard tells us whether the grinder fits before we quote.

Suffolk

Suffolk is west of Chesapeake and the largest city in Virginia by land, with a lot of farmland and woods between the neighborhoods. Send the address so we know which end of Suffolk we are headed to.

Portsmouth

Portsmouth is across the Elizabeth River from Norfolk and next door to Chesapeake. Text the address and a photo of the stump or the yard, and we go from there.

Hampton

Hampton is on the Peninsula, across the water from Norfolk by the Hampton Roads Bridge-Tunnel. We do come across. Text the address first so we can plan the trip and the day.

Just outside one of these cities?

We also work in the towns and neighborhoods around these cities. Text the address and we will tell you whether we can get there.

What to send.

  • The address, or the neighborhood if you would rather.
  • A photo of the stump or the yard.
  • A photo of the way in for the machine.
