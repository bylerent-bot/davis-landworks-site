Privacy

What we keep when you ask for an estimate.

What you put in the form goes to Harrison so he can answer you. It’s never sold or shared for anyone’s marketing.

What the form keeps.

Your name, your mobile number, the job address, the service you picked, the size if you gave one, the best day for the work, anything you wrote in the details box, and any photos you added. The form also notes which website or search sent you to us, so we know where our work comes from.

What it is used for.

Answering your request. Harrison reads it, texts or calls you back with any questions, and gives you a price. To estimate how far a job is from us, we look up the job address with the U.S. Census Bureau's free address geocoder. That is the whole use.

Photos.

Photos are made smaller on your phone or computer before they are sent, to about 1600 pixels on the long side. That step also strips the location and camera details a photo carries. We keep the photos with your request.

On a phone.

On a phone, the form opens a text message to Harrison for you to send. We also keep a copy of what you typed into the form so he can follow up. If you skip the form and just text or call, nothing is kept on this site unless Harrison adds your job to his records himself.

How long we keep it.

We keep your request while it may be needed for your job. If you hire Harrison, the job and payment records are kept for seven years for tax purposes. If you want your details removed, text or call Harrison and he will have them taken out, except what the payment record for a job you paid for has to keep.

What the form keeps.

Right now, nothing. On a phone, the form opens a text message to Harrison for you to send. On a computer, it shows you his number and an email link. The only copy of your request is the text or email you send him, and this site keeps none of it.

Photos.

Photos go in your text or email, straight to Harrison. Nothing is uploaded to this site.
