The stumps we get called about.

Some stumps sit in the open. Some are up against a fence or next to a buried line. Send a photo and we will tell you what we can do.

  • The stump in the middle of the lawn that you keep mowing around.
  • Stumps grown into a chain-link fence line.
  • Stumps near underground power lines, where the work has to be careful.
  • A stump in a garden bed you want to plant again.
  • A few stumps at once.

What the spot looks like after.

We grind the stump down and clean up the work area when the job is done. You can seed or plant over the spot. If the rest of the yard needs leveling too, we do that as well.

Getting the machine in.

It helps to see the way in: the gate, the side yard, anything between the street and the stump. Add that photo when you text, along with a photo of the stump itself.
